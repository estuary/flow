## Goal
The one document PLAN's "Report" section describes, and the go/no-go.

## Read
- PLAN.md: "Report" and "Decision".
- Every `spike/report/exp*.md`, `spike/report/libkrun-exposure.md`, and
  STATUS.md end to end.

## May touch
`spike/report/REPORT.md` and, for the copy that leaves the repo, a
Markdown draft handed back in chat (keyboard-typeable characters only, no
em dashes, no arrows other than `->`).

## Contents, in order
1. Decision: go, or the specific redesign a failed gate forces, per PLAN.
2. Gate table: experiment, pass/fail, one sentence where it matters.
3. Tables from experiments 2, 5, 9, 10, copied not summarized.
4. What phase 2 starts from: the exact `podman run` line, the nft ruleset
   (`flow-sandbox-egress --print` output), the libkrun call sequence from
   the shim, the mkfs options, the measured overhead constant, the THP
   result, the layer-vs-share implication from experiment 5.
5. Anything that only worked from a root shell on the host.
6. The accepted costs: blocked-connection latency under drop, root DAX
   unavailable on libkrun 1.19, anything else STATUS recorded.
7. Open problems, each with a proposed owner. Include: the AWS instance
   family question (out of scope here, still open), and any libkrun
   exposure finding that is not "none found".
